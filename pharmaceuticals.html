Pharmaceuticals

Specialty chemical sourcing and pharmaceutical chemistry applications.

Overview

PURETi TiPT is used as a process chemical in the manufacture of certain prazoles, including pantoprazole, omeprazole, lansoprazole and rabeprazole. It is used during synthesis, not as an ingredient intentionally included in the final drug formulation.

Pharmaceutical synthesis applications

PURETi TiPT

Titanium isopropoxide is used as a catalyst or reagent in organic synthesis, particularly enolate formation and condensation reactions.

Role in prazoles synthesis

Applications described by Polygel Global include catalyzing cyclization reactions, promoting selective oxidation or rearrangement, and acting as a mild Lewis acid to enable specific bond formations.

According to the supplied application material, the titanium species is removed during purification and is not expected to remain in the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) in significant quantities. Residual content and compliance require process-specific analytical verification.
