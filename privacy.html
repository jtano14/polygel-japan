Privacy Policy

How Polygel Japan handles personal information and website analytics.

Draft for review. Effective date to be confirmed before launch.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Polygel Japan Co., Ltd. handles personal information submitted through this website, including business inquiries and technical document requests. It applies to this website and not automatically to other Polygel Group companies or third-party websites.

2. Information we collect

When inquiry services are activated, we may collect your name, company, business email address, country or region, inquiry category, message and information you choose to provide. We may also collect limited technical information necessary to operate and protect the website. Optional website analytics will be used only with your consent.

3. Purposes of use

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, provide requested product or application information, evaluate business relationships, coordinate with relevant personnel or Group entities when necessary to respond, maintain security and improve website services.

4. Sharing and service providers

Where needed to address your request, information may be shared with appropriate Polygel Group personnel, business partners or service providers. The planned inquiry service is Formspree; the website and email hosting are planned through Sakura Internet. These services will be configured and their processing arrangements confirmed before the inquiry form is activated. Information may be processed outside Japan when necessary for an international inquiry.

5. Cookies and Google Analytics

We use essential browser storage to remember your cookie preference. We plan to use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and visitor interactions, but its analytics scripts will not load unless you accept optional analytics cookies. You may reject or change your choice at any time using Cookie Settings in the website footer. Google Analytics will not be activated until a measurement ID is configured.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss and misuse. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, applicable legal obligations and legitimate business records. Specific retention periods will be finalized when the inquiry service is configured.

7. Your inquiries and requests

You may contact Polygel Japan to request information about how your personal information is handled or to ask for correction or deletion where applicable. The official privacy contact email will be published before personal-information submissions are enabled.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated as our services, applicable requirements or processing arrangements change. The current version will be posted on this page.

Contact
