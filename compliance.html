Environment, Health & Safety

Polygel Industries Pvt. Ltd. states its commitment to protecting the environment and maintaining a healthy and safe workplace. Its environmental, health and safety system covers policies, training, planning, audits and inspections, with an emphasis on continuous improvement.

These commitments describe Polygel Group operations. They do not independently establish certification of Polygel Japan.
