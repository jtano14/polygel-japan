COATINGS APPLICATION

PUREti Organo-Titanates for Industrial Metal Coatings

Specialty chemicals for industrial, automotive, architectural, protective and anti-corrosive coatings.

Polygel's specialty chemicals are integral to high-performance coating systems. The products are formulated to deliver excellent adhesion, durability, corrosion resistance and long-term stability across industrial coatings, automotive coatings, architectural coatings and protective and anti-corrosive coatings.

PUREti grades of titanates improve adhesion to metallic substrates through covalent bonding, cross-linking functional groups of a wide variety of resin binders and acting as coupling agents for pigments and fillers. This allows formulators to develop coatings for diverse binder systems with the adhesion and anti-corrosion properties required in general industrial, chemical and transport industries.

Performance benefits

  • Exceptional adhesion to metals, plastics and composites
  • Enhanced chemical resistance, including corrosion and moisture protection
  • Improved surface appearance, gloss, leveling and flow
  • Catalysis of curing reactions in PU, epoxy and acrylic systems
  • High-temperature and UV stability
  • Increased mechanical strength and wear resistance

Coatings & rheology

  • Adhesion promoter for inks
  • Rheology modifiers for paints
  • Anti-corrosion coatings
  • Glass coatings
