POLYGEL JAPAN / APPLICATIONS

Coatings

Organo-titanates for industrial coatings, adhesion promotion and corrosion protection.

Overview

Polygel's organo-titanates are used in industrial, automotive, architectural and protective coatings. They can promote adhesion to metallic substrates through covalent bonding, support resin cross-linking and act as coupling agents for pigments and fillers. Published performance benefits include improved chemical resistance, surface appearance, curing, heat stability and mechanical strength.

Application solutions

Adhesion & durability

Adhesion to metals, plastics and composites; enhanced mechanical strength and wear resistance.

Corrosion & moisture protection

Improved chemical resistance and protection in industrial coatings.

Coatings & rheology

Applications include ink adhesion promotion, paint rheology modification, anti-corrosion coatings and glass coatings.
