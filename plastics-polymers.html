POLYGEL JAPAN / APPLICATIONS

Plastics & Polymers

Antioxidants, light stabilizers and specialty additives for polymer processing.

Overview

Polygel offers antioxidants, UV/light stabilizers and specialty processing additives for PVC, HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE and other thermoplastics. The range supports thermal stability, resistance to yellowing and embrittlement, and long-term aging performance. Product formats include powders, flakes and granules; custom blends may be available.

Application solutions

Antioxidants

Support processing stability and protection against oxidation-related degradation.

UV absorbers & HALS

Support polymer protection against light-related degradation.

Specialty processing additives

Products selected according to polymer system and processing requirements.
