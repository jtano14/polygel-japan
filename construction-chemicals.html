Construction Chemicals

Chemical and mineral admixture solutions for concrete and construction formulations.

Overview

Polygel manufactures chemical and mineral admixture systems for ready-mix concrete, supporting workability and slump retention across concrete grades including M-20 to M-100. Applications include roads, bridges, flyovers, metro railways, dams and irrigation projects.
