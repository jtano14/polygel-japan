POLYGEL JAPAN / APPLICATIONS

Catalysts

Titanium, zirconium, vanadium and metal alkoxide catalyst solutions.

Overview

Polygel offers homogeneous metal catalysts for polymerization, esterification, adhesive formulation, inks, coatings and fine chemical synthesis. The published range includes titanium, zirconium and vanadium catalysts as well as oligomerization co-catalysts and polycondensation catalysts.

Application solutions

Titanium catalysts

High-reactivity catalysts for esterification, transesterification, polymerization and surface adhesion.

Zirconium catalysts

Applications include chemical processing, catalysts, coatings and zirconium compound production.

Vanadium catalysts

Oxidation catalysis and polyolefin polymerization; the source references VOCl3 and CAB catalysts.

Oligomerization co-catalysts

PURETi HP for alpha-olefin production and triethylaluminium in olefin polymerization systems.

Polycondensation catalysts

PURETi CAT-25 is identified in Polygel Global's PBAT / polycondensation catalyst section.
